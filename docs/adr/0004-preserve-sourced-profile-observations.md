# Preserve sourced Profile observations

Humans will preserve conflicting sourced Observations rather than allowing enrichment providers to overwrite Member-controlled Profile truth. Member Statements take precedence. For an imported, unclaimed Profile, trusted provider evidence may materialize a display field such as the current company while the sourced Observation remains the evidence of record. Provider failures leave the last successful Observation marked stale, and complete provider snapshots expire after 30 days while normalized Observations retain source, collection time, pipeline version, and confidence.
